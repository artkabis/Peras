# The garden of Apeiron — first run, kept with its flaw

_This is the first run under the protocol of decision 0013, kept as it came out. Its measure of H2 is flawed: for some zoomed tiles, no AVIF in 4:2:0 reached an SSIM of 0.95, the search gave no size, written "Infinity", and the script counted that as H2 holding. H2 was in fact undetermined by this run. The method was corrected, the hypotheses unchanged; the [results](../RESULTS.md) are those of the corrected run, and the correction is listed there as a departure._

Generated by `node scripts/bench-garden.mjs` under the protocol of [decision 0013](../../docs/en/decisions/0013-garden-of-apeiron.md), registered before these measures. Machine: Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz. Renders of 1024 × 1024 pixels. Every number is also in [results.json](results.json).
